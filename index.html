Semester Reflection

What I learned: Over the course of this semester, I developed a much stronger understanding of digital culture while also finding ways to bring my creativity into the work. One of my favorite parts of the labs was the reflection sections because they gave me the opportunity to slow down and think about the process behind each project. Writing about the steps I took allowed me to explain how I approached each assignment and what I learned along the way. Something I really enjoyed about this class was the creative freedom we had with designing our own websites. Being able to customize the layout, colors, and overall feel of my page made the projects more fun and personal. One small feature I added that I loved was a “celebration button.” I created it during a moment when I was feeling especially stressed while working through one of the labs. Adding something lighthearted to the site helped remind me that the process of learning something new can still be fun. It also made my website feel more like my own space rather than just another assignment. I also learned a lot about working with data and presenting it in ways that others can easily understand. Using Tableau introduced me to new ways of analyzing information and turning it into visualizations that tell a story. I chose a dataset that interested me and practiced cleaning and organizing the information so that it could be displayed more clearly. This experience helped me understand how important it is to make data accessible and easy for others to interpret.

Challenges I overcame: At the beginning of the semester, one of the biggest challenges for me was understanding HTML with very little prior understanding. The coding language was newer to me, and it took time to understand how everything worked together,

Going forward, I hope to use the skills I learned in this class during my football internship, especially when it comes to tracking analytics for the team’s media accounts. Learning how to organize and visualize data will help me understand what content performs well and how fans engage with it. I’m excited to use both the creative and analytical skills from this class in a real-world setting.
