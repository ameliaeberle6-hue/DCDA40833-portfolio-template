Project Overview

For lab 5 I made a toggle between dark and light mode, and I also created a celebration button where cofetti comes down when you hit the button. lastly I got AI to fix my photo sizes.

Reflection

Lab 5 was when I started actually having fun with the site instead of just trying to get things done. One of the main things I added was a dark mode / light mode toggle, which made the site feel more interactive and less flat. It also helped me understand how changing styles in one place affects the entire site, so I had to be more organized with my CSS. I also added a celebration button that drops confetti when you click it. At first it was just meant to be a fun extra, but I ended up using it every time I finished something on the site. It became this small reward I actually looked forward to, especially when I was stuck or working late. It helped make the whole process feel less frustrating and more motivating, even if it was just confetti on a screen. Lastly, I used AI to fix my photo sizes because resizing everything by hand was getting old. Using AI made the images more consistent and saved time, which made the site look cleaner overall. This part of the lab reflects how I like to use tools that make the process easier without overcomplicating things. .
