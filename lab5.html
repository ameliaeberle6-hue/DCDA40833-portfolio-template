Lab 5 — Interactive Visualization

Project Overview

For lab 5 I made a toggle between dark and light mode, and I also created a celebration button where cofetti comes down when you hit the button. lastly I got AI to fix my photo sizes.

Reflection

Lab 5 was when I started actually enjoying working on the site instead of just trying to get through it. One of the main things I added was a dark mode / light mode toggle, which made the site feel more interactive and less flat. It also forced me to be more organized with my CSS, since changing one thing suddenly affected the whole site. I also added a celebration button that drops confetti when you click it. At first it was just a fun extra, but I ended up hitting it every time I finally finished something on the site. It became this small reward I actually looked forward to, especially after struggling through a problem. It made the process feel lighter and honestly helped keep me motivated. Lastly, I used AI to fix my photo sizes, and that part took way longer than I expected. At first, I was way too broad with my prompts and didn’t really understand how to communicate what I wanted. At one point, the AI deleted all of my work, so I basically had to restart from ground zero. After a lot of trial and error (and some frustration), I figured out how to be more specific and use the tool correctly. Once it worked, it saved a ton of time and made the site look cleaner overall. That experience taught me that AI isn’t magic, it still takes patience, clear instructions, and a lot of testing. Overall, Lab 5 shows that I’m more comfortable experimenting, problem-solving, and sticking with things even when they don’t work the first time.
